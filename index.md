# Mockmint Marketing Privacy Policy

**Effective date: 11 October 2026**

Mockmint Marketing is operated by Mockmint to create, review, schedule and manage content for its Facebook Page. This policy explains what information the application processes, how it is used, where it is stored and how to request its deletion.

**Privacy contact:** tofazzolshuvo.up@gmail.com

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to the Mockmint Marketing application and its Facebook integration, content planning dashboard and AI-assisted content tools.

The application currently supports Mockmint’s own marketing activities through an authenticated owner dashboard. It is not a public account-registration service.

Facebook, GitHub and connected AI providers operate their own services under their respective privacy policies.

## 2. Information We Process

Depending on the features used and permissions granted, we may process:

- Facebook account identifiers and basic profile information supplied during authorization, such as a name and profile picture.
- Facebook Page identifiers, names and the authorized user’s Page management tasks.
- Access tokens and permission information needed to access the authorized Page.
- Page posts, captions, images, post identifiers, publication status, scheduled publication times and links to published posts.
- Available Page engagement information, including reaction and comment counts, and Page insights when requested.
- User-generated Page content, such as comments, when accessed through an authorized feature.
- Brand information entered in the dashboard, including services, target audiences, tone, website details, logos and content restrictions.
- Chat messages, prompts, uploaded files, generated captions, generated images, content plans and approval records.
- Operational information, including application sessions, request counts, errors and publication logs.

Permission to access a category of information does not mean that we continuously collect all information in that category.

We do not request Facebook account passwords. Facebook authorization takes place through Meta’s authorization process.

## 3. How We Use Information

We use information to:

- Connect the application to an authorized Facebook Page.
- Generate and revise marketing topics, captions and images.
- Remember brand preferences and prepare weekly or monthly content plans.
- Allow the owner to review and approve content.
- Submit approved posts and schedules to Facebook.
- Verify scheduling and publication results and reduce duplicate posting.
- Display available engagement information.
- Maintain application security, troubleshoot problems and manage usage limits.
- Respond to privacy and deletion requests.

AI generation alone does not authorize publication. Posts require an explicit approval action before the application submits them for publication or scheduling.

## 4. Facebook Access and Permissions

The application may use the following Facebook permissions:

- **public_profile:** basic account identification during authorization.
- **pages_show_list:** identifying Pages the authorized account manages.
- **pages_read_engagement:** reading supported Page content and engagement information.
- **pages_read_user_content:** reading supported user-generated content on the authorized Page.
- **pages_manage_posts:** creating and managing authorized Page posts.
- **read_insights:** reading supported Page insights.
- **business_management:** accessing relevant business assets where required by the configured integration.

Access is limited by the permissions granted, Meta’s requirements and the application’s enabled features. We do not use these permissions to manage unrelated Pages without authorization.

Approved scheduled posts may be stored on Facebook and published by Facebook even when the local dashboard is offline.

## 5. AI Processing

When an AI feature is used, relevant prompts, brand information, conversation context, draft content and selected attachments may be sent to the configured AI provider.

Supported connections may include OpenAI through ChatGPT/Codex or the OpenAI API, Google Gemini and Anthropic Claude. The provider used depends on the application’s configuration.

These providers process submitted information under their own terms, privacy policies and applicable account settings. We do not promise that every provider or account type has the same retention or model-training practices.

For the manual “copy prompt and open ChatGPT” workflow, the user chooses what to paste or upload into ChatGPT. Content uploaded back into the dashboard is stored as application content.

Facebook access tokens and AI credentials are not intentionally included in content-generation prompts.

Users should avoid submitting sensitive personal information or confidential material that is unnecessary for creating marketing content.

## 6. Sharing and Public Content

We do not sell personal information.

Information may be shared with:

- **Meta**, to authorize Page access, retrieve supported Page information and submit approved posts and schedules.
- **The configured AI provider**, to perform requested generation or editing.
- **Infrastructure providers**, where needed to host or operate the application.
- **Authorities or other recipients**, when disclosure is required by applicable law.

Approved captions and images published to Facebook become visible according to Facebook’s audience and platform settings. Public posts may be viewed, copied or shared by others.

## 7. Storage and Security

The dashboard currently runs on a local server using persistent database and media storage. Application access requires owner authentication.

Security measures include password hashing, authenticated access to private media, encrypted storage for configured integration credentials and approval checks for publication.

These measures reduce risk but cannot guarantee absolute security.

The privacy-policy page is hosted separately on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process connection information, such as IP addresses, when someone visits that page, under GitHub’s own privacy practices. Hosting this policy does not itself upload the dashboard’s private database, files or credentials to GitHub.

## 8. Retention

We retain brand settings, conversations, drafts, uploaded files, generated media, content plans and operational records while they are needed for the application’s operation, troubleshooting or recordkeeping.

The application does not currently apply a fixed automatic deletion period to every category of stored information.

Information may be removed following a verified deletion request, when it is no longer needed or when the application is discontinued, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Information sent to Meta or an AI provider may remain subject to that provider’s retention rules.

## 9. Access, Correction and Data Deletion

To request access, correction or deletion of information held by Mockmint Marketing, email:

**tofazzolshuvo.up@gmail.com**

For deletion requests, use the subject:

**Mockmint Marketing — Data Deletion Request**

Describe the information or account involved. Where relevant, include your Facebook Page URL or Page ID so we can identify the records.

Do not send passwords, access tokens or other authentication secrets.

We may request reasonable information to verify that you are authorized to make the request. After verification, we will review the request and remove applicable information under our control, except where retention is necessary to meet legal obligations or resolve an outstanding issue. We will communicate the outcome through the contact address provided.

Deleting local application records does not automatically delete posts or schedules already stored on Facebook. Those posts and schedules must also be managed through Facebook or Meta Business Suite.

Requests concerning information retained independently by Meta, GitHub or an AI provider should also be directed to that provider.

## 10. Revoking Facebook Access

You may revoke the application’s access through Facebook’s Business Integrations settings.

Revocation prevents further authorized access through the revoked connection but does not automatically delete previously stored local records or already-published Facebook content.

To request deletion of local records, follow the instructions above.

## 11. Cookies and Sessions

The dashboard uses an authentication session cookie to keep the owner signed in. The application does not intentionally use advertising cookies.

Facebook login, AI-provider websites and GitHub Pages may use their own cookies or similar technologies under their respective policies.

## 12. International Processing

Connected service providers may process information in countries other than the user’s country of residence. Their applicable terms and privacy policies describe their processing arrangements.

## 13. Children’s Privacy

Mockmint Marketing is intended for business administration and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly seek personal information from children.

If you believe a child’s personal information has been submitted to the application, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

## 14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the application’s features, providers or data practices change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
